# RandomX third-party notices

The x86-64 Zen 4 parity JIT and AArch64 VM/dataset JIT under `upstream/`
contain RandomX code distributed with XMRig. The 512-bit VAES lane-packing
strategy in `rx/aes.cpp`, and the redundant-CFROUND analysis, reciprocal
calculation, and executable-code layout ideas in `rx/jit_x86.cpp`, are also
adapted from that implementation.
